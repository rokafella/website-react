TrackMyScore · Privacy Policy

We don't store your data. Period.

No data from your App ever leaves your device — not even the logs. TrackMyScore is designed to work entirely on-device. Your scores, history, and any personal information you enter stay local to your phone.

We do not collect, transmit, or store any data on external servers. There are no analytics, no crash reporting that sends data off-device, and no advertising identifiers.

For any questions or concerns, contact info@rohitkapoor.dev.
